The sound effects in public/audio/sfx/ are built by tools/audio.mjs from these packs, all
released under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- Kenney: RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds (kenney.nl)
- rubberduck: 80 CC0 RPG SFX, 100 CC0 SFX, 80 CC0 creature SFX (opengameart.org)
- Swishes Sound Pack (opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack)
- 150 Combat Sound Effects, a CC0 compilation (opengameart.org/content/150-combat-sound-effects):
  spell impacts by lentikula, blasts by CoMiGo, explosions from Kenney Sci-fi Sounds
- Cure Magic (opengameart.org/content/cure-magic)
- Ice spells (opengameart.org/content/ice-spells)
- Freeze Spell (opengameart.org/content/freeze-spell-0)
- Magic Words + Healing Sound Effect (opengameart.org/content/magic-words-healing-sound-effect)
